COURSEWORK & ENGINEERING PROJECTS

01Residential electrical & UPS designMATLAB · MULTISIM+

A complete home wiring design for six rooms plus a garage/laundry, with UPS backup. MATLAB load, current, and power calculations supported the NI Multisim simulation, with a 21.5 kW main supply and 1.6 kW UPS.

02DC power supply designCIRCUITS · LAB WORK+

A regulated approximately 2.70 V circuit using a full-wave rectifier and four 1N4007 diodes as a shunt regulator. I analyzed line/load regulation and ripple, verified behavior with an oscilloscope, and documented the work in LaTeX.

03Inventory management systemC++ · MODULAR DESIGN+

A C++ console application for adding, updating, and searching products, recording sales, and calculating total inventory value. Built using structs and modular design.
