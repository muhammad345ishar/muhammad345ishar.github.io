About

I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering student at PIEAS — the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences — and most of what I know outside the syllabus came from breaking something and then having to fix it.

Coursework keeps me in circuits and mathematics: BJT and MOSFET amplifier analysis, DC power supply design, applied physics, calculus, linear algebra, differential equations. Away from the coursework I build — a local LLM stack running on my own hardware, a Python service that watches 450+ WhatsApp contacts and answers for me, a C++ game I ported off Windows because I wanted to play it on a Mac.

I come from a farming family in Wazirabad, which is where the patience for slow work comes from. I stay in community work through Scouting and Youth Force Talwara.
