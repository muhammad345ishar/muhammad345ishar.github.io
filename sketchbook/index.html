COURSEWORK & ENGINEERING PROJECTS
01Residential electrical & UPS design+

Home wiring for six rooms plus a garage/laundry, with UPS backup. MATLAB load, current, and power calculations supported NI Multisim simulation, sized to a 21.5 kW main supply and a 1.6 kW UPS.

02DC power supply design+

A regulated approximately 2.70 V circuit using a full-wave rectifier and four 1N4007 diodes as a shunt regulator. Line/load regulation and ripple were analyzed and checked with an oscilloscope, with a LaTeX report.

03Inventory management system+

A C++ console application for adding, updating, and searching products, recording sales, and calculating total inventory value, using structs and modular design.
